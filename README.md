# 🌌 50 Days • 50 Tech Thoughts

> Technology isn't just algorithms and code.  
> It's emotions hidden behind logic, stories behind systems, and maybe... a little poetry behind every innovation.

Welcome to **50 Days • 50 Tech Thoughts** — a personal 50-day journey exploring technology from a slightly different perspective.

For 50 days, I challenged myself to write **one original tech-inspired thought, reflection, or poem every day**, connecting concepts from computer science with the way we think, feel, communicate, and experience life.

Because sometimes...

> A programming language can teach us about communication.  
> Memory can teach us about what we choose to remember.  
> Data can tell the story of who we are.  
> And a database can remind us that even chaos can have a place.

---

## 💭 Why this project?

Technology is often explained through definitions, diagrams, algorithms, and code.

But I wanted to ask:

**What if we looked at technology a little differently?**

What if the concepts we study every day could also tell us something about people, emotions, life, and the way we see the world?

What if computer science wasn't just something we learned for exams, assignments, or projects, but something we could connect with beyond the classroom?

So, this project is my attempt to explore the **human side of technology** — one concept and one thought at a time.

## ❤️ A Little Reflection

What started as an idea to write about technology every day became something more than a collection of technical concepts.

It gave me a reason to look at familiar topics differently, connect what I study with what I write, and find stories in places where I usually see only logic.

Fifty days later, I don't think technology and creativity are two separate worlds anymore.

Sometimes, they meet in the most unexpected ways.

And that's what this journey was about.
